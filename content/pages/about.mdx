# Hi, I'm Nathan.

I'm Nathan Puls, PMHNP-BC, a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner providing psychiatric evaluations and medication management for adults with anxiety, depression, and ADHD.

Before becoming a nurse practitioner, I worked as a psychiatric nurse at The Menninger Clinic. I earned my master's degree from the University of Texas at Tyler and became board certified in 2025.

I speak **English and Spanish** and believe good mental health care starts with listening, understanding the person behind the symptoms, and working together on a practical treatment plan.
